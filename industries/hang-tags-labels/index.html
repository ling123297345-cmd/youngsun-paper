Hang Tags & Labels Paper Solutions

A hang tag or label is small, but it carries price, product information and brand identity at the point of purchase. The right paper must hold fine print, clean die-cut edges and premium finishes while matching the product and packaging around it.

Performance Priorities

Clean edges

Through-dyed and well-converted papers keep punched and die-cut edges presentable.

Fine detail

Stable surfaces support small typography, barcodes, foil and embossing.

Brand match

Coordinate color, texture and finish with apparel, jewelry or beverage packaging.

Recommended Paper Grades

  • Black Paper - Deep-black paper and cardboard for luxury packaging, hang tags, stationery, printing and creative converting.
  • Label Paper - Premium label and tag face stock — smooth, printable, and engineered for high-speed converting and adhesive coating.
  • Soft Touch Paper - Velvet-smooth tactile coated paper — the sensation of luxury that makes people stop and touch.
  • Pearlescent Paper - Light-reflecting iridescent paper with a pearl-like shimmer — elegance that moves with the light.
  • Embossed Paper - Three-dimensional textured paper — raised patterns that turn packaging into a tactile brand experience.
  • Gold & Silver Card Paper - Brilliant metallic gold and silver card paper with mirror-like reflective surface — for luxury packaging, gift boxes, greeting cards, and high-end brand presentation.

Buyer Checklist

  • Tag or label size, shape and hole position
  • GSM, stiffness and desired edge color
  • Printing, barcode and variable-data requirements
  • Foil, embossing, debossing or lamination
  • Adhesive, string, eyelet or bottle-neck construction
  • Color tolerance, samples and repeat-order control

Available Processing

  • Die-cutting
  • Hole punching
  • Embossing
  • Foil compatibility
  • Custom color
