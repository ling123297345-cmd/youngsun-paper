CKB (Coated Kraft Back) Overview

YOUNGSUN Coated Kraft Back Board (CKB) has a white triple-coated printing face and an unbleached kraft reverse. The reference range is 200–350 gsm, supplied in sheets or reels subject to grade and size. It is considered for printed beverage carriers, dry-goods cartons and retail folding cartons where both the visible print surface and reverse-side appearance matter. For chilled or frozen distribution, confirm the exact board construction, any required barrier or laminate, the filling conditions and finished-carton performance by trial. Food-contact suitability is not established by the CKB name and must be documented for the intended market and contact conditions. Request a physical sample to compare printing, creasing, folding and glue bonding before production.

Specifications

  • Grammage: 200 – 350 gsm
  • Surface: Triple-coated white top with unbleached kraft back
  • Supply forms: Sheets, reels, custom sizes
  • Common weights: 200, 235, 245, 255, 280, 300, 325 and 350 gsm
  • Grade: CKB; confirm the quoted grade's technical sheet

Applications

  • Beverage packaging
  • Dry food cartons
  • Secondary cartons for chilled or frozen goods, subject to pack validation
  • Toy boxes
  • Shoe boxes
  • Tool packaging

Frequently Asked Questions

How is CKB different from folding box board or duplex board?

CKB combines a coated white printing face with an unbleached kraft reverse for strength and a natural kraft appearance. FBB and duplex board use different fibre and back constructions. Compare stiffness, tear performance, reverse-side appearance, printing and the final carton environment before selection.

Is CKB suitable for frozen or chilled food packaging?

CKB can be selected for beverage, chilled and frozen-food cartons when the complete structure matches moisture, temperature, filling and storage conditions. Food-contact and barrier suitability must be confirmed for the exact grade, coating or laminate and destination market.

What should I specify when requesting CKB?

Send grammage, sheet or reel dimensions, quantity per size, carton design, printing and gluing process, storage conditions, any food-contact or barrier requirement and destination port. The reference MOQ starts from 1 metric ton for a regular grade and size, subject to specification confirmation; standard samples are free and the customer pays international courier charges.
