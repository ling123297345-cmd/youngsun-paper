Packaging & Printing

Packaging performance starts with matching the board structure to the pack format, printing method and converting line. YOUNGSUN helps box makers, printers and packaging distributors compare structural boards, printable boards and wrapping papers as one coordinated sourcing program.

Recommended paper grades

  • Grey Board - FSC-certified recycled grey board for rigid boxes, bookbinding, stationery, puzzles and industrial packaging.
  • Folding Box Board - C1S ivory board with a smooth coated surface, high brightness and reliable folding performance for printed cartons.
  • Duplex Board - Coated white-top duplex board with grey back for economical folding cartons and printed packaging.
  • Kraft Paper - Natural kraft paper for sacks, bags, wrapping, industrial packaging and paper converting.

Food & Beverage

Food packaging paper must balance forming performance, grease or moisture resistance, printability and the compliance documents required for the destination market. We help converters shortlist suitable base paper and barrier structures for cups, wraps, bags and takeaway containers.

Recommended paper grades

  • Cup Paper - Food-grade cupstock base board for paper cups, food containers and PE-coated packaging.
  • PE Coated Paper - Polyethylene-laminated paper and board for moisture barrier, heat-seal, and liquid-resistant packaging.
  • Greaseproof Paper - White and natural greaseproof paper for bakery, fast food, takeaway and food wrapping applications.
  • Silicone Coated Paper - Premium release liner and non-stick baking paper — silicone-coated for clean release every time.

Publishing & Stationery

Publishing papers must deliver stable color, opacity, bulk and press performance while controlling finished weight and cost. We supply coated and uncoated grades in sheets or reels for books, notebooks, catalogs and commercial stationery.

Recommended paper grades

  • Woodfree Paper - Uncoated woodfree paper with high brightness, good opacity and stable runnability for books, notebooks and commercial printing.
  • C2S Art Board Card - Coated-two-sides art board with bright white surface and smooth finish — for premium print and high-end card applications.
  • LWC Paper (Lightweight Coated) - Lightweight coated paper for magazines, catalogs, inserts, manuals and high-volume commercial printing.
  • Color Offset Paper - Lightweight colored offset paper for office forms, inserts, publishing, stationery and promotional printing.
