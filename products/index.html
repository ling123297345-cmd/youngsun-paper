Package Board

Your comprehensive source for durable structural packaging. Our extensive range covers Grey Board, Black Cardboard, Folding Box Board (FBB), C2S Art Board, Kraft Paper, and Duplex Board to meet all your rigid box and container needs.

  • Grey Board - FSC-certified recycled grey board for rigid boxes, bookbinding, stationery, puzzles and industrial packaging.
  • Black Paper - Deep-black paper and cardboard for luxury packaging, hang tags, stationery, printing and creative converting.
  • Color Card Paper - Colored card paper for packaging, hang tags, stationery, invitations and creative printing.
  • Folding Box Board - C1S ivory board with a smooth coated surface, high brightness and reliable folding performance for printed cartons.
  • C2S Art Board Card - Coated-two-sides art board with bright white surface and smooth finish — for premium print and high-end card applications.
  • Kraft Paper - Natural kraft paper for sacks, bags, wrapping, industrial packaging and paper converting.
  • Duplex Board - Coated white-top duplex board with grey back for economical folding cartons and printed packaging.
  • PE Coated Paper - Polyethylene-laminated paper and board for moisture barrier, heat-seal, and liquid-resistant packaging.
  • CKB (Coated Kraft Back) - High-strength coated kraft back board for beverage, food, frozen, retail and industrial folding cartons.

Culture Paper

We supply Woodfree Paper, Color Offset Paper, LWC Paper, and NCR Paper, ensuring crisp, consistent results for your brochures, books, and professional stationery.

  • Woodfree Paper - Uncoated woodfree paper with high brightness, good opacity and stable runnability for books, notebooks and commercial printing.
  • Color Offset Paper - Lightweight colored offset paper for office forms, inserts, publishing, stationery and promotional printing.
  • LWC Paper (Lightweight Coated) - Lightweight coated paper for magazines, catalogs, inserts, manuals and high-volume commercial printing.
  • NCR Paper (Carbonless Paper) - Self-copying carbonless paper for forms, invoices, and receipts — no carbon sheet needed, clean and smudge-free.
  • Copy Paper - Office copy paper in multiple grammages, sheet formats and packing options for distributors, institutions and private-label buyers.
  • Newsprint - Cost-efficient, high-speed printing paper for newspapers, advertising inserts, and mass-circulation publications.
  • A4 Copy Paper - Standard A4 office copy paper — bright white, jam-free, and laser/inkjet compatible in bulk packaging for export.
  • A4 Thermal Paper - A4-sized thermal paper for portable and mobile printing — no ink, no toner, just heat. Ideal for on-the-go professionals.
  • Thermal Paper Roll - Small-format thermal paper rolls for POS terminals, receipt printers, and instant photo printers — crisp images, reliable performance.
  • Tracing Paper - Translucent tracing paper for luxury packaging, printing, drawing, invitations and decorative applications.
