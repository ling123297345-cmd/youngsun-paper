Gift presentation

Gift Wrapping & Decoration Paper Solutions

Gift presentation combines wrapping, bags, cards and protective liners into one coordinated experience. YOUNGSUN supplies lightweight tissue, strong bag paper and decorative card grades that help retailers, converters and brands build consistent seasonal collections.

Performance Priorities

Color system

Build coordinated wrap, tissue, bag and card combinations around the brand palette.

Handling

Balance foldability, tear resistance and stiffness for each presentation component.

Seasonal repeatability

Control shade, texture and finish across repeat orders and seasonal programs.

Recommended Paper Grades

  • Color Tissue Paper - Vibrant translucent tissue in a rainbow palette — the finishing touch that transforms packaging into an experience.
  • Kraft Paper - Natural kraft paper for sacks, bags, wrapping, industrial packaging and paper converting.
  • MG White Kraft Paper - Premium machine-glazed white kraft paper with a glossy printable face and glue-ready reverse for food wrapping, bags and flexible packaging.
  • Color Card Paper - Colored card paper for packaging, hang tags, stationery, invitations and creative printing.
  • Embossed Paper - Three-dimensional textured paper — raised patterns that turn packaging into a tactile brand experience.
  • Tracing Paper - Translucent tracing paper for luxury packaging, printing, drawing, invitations and decorative applications.

Buyer Checklist

  • Finished item: wrap, bag, card, liner or insert
  • GSM, stiffness, foldability and tear requirement
  • Color palette, texture and seasonal consistency
  • Printing, metallic, embossing or translucent effect
  • Sheet, reel, tissue format and packing method
  • Collection volume and repeat-order schedule

Available Processing

  • Custom color
  • Sheet cutting
  • Reel slitting
  • Embossing
  • Retail packing
