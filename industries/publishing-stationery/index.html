Print and publishing

Publishing & Stationery Paper Solutions

Publishing papers must deliver stable color, opacity, bulk and press performance while controlling finished weight and cost. We supply coated and uncoated grades in sheets or reels for books, notebooks, catalogs and commercial stationery.

Performance Priorities

Image reproduction

Surface and coating selected for text, photography or mixed content.

Opacity and bulk

Control show-through, page feel, spine width and mailing weight.

Press performance

Stable moisture, clean cutting and reliable high-speed feeding.

Recommended Paper Grades

  • Woodfree Paper - Uncoated woodfree paper with high brightness, good opacity and stable runnability for books, notebooks and commercial printing.
  • C2S Art Board Card - Coated-two-sides art board with bright white surface and smooth finish — for premium print and high-end card applications.
  • LWC Paper (Lightweight Coated) - Lightweight coated paper for magazines, catalogs, inserts, manuals and high-volume commercial printing.
  • Color Offset Paper - Lightweight colored offset paper for office forms, inserts, publishing, stationery and promotional printing.
  • Grey Board - FSC-certified recycled grey board for rigid boxes, bookbinding, stationery, puzzles and industrial packaging.
  • NCR Paper (Carbonless Paper) - Self-copying carbonless paper for forms, invoices, and receipts — no carbon sheet needed, clean and smudge-free.

Buyer Checklist

  • Publication type, page count and finished format
  • Printing process and target image quality
  • GSM, opacity, brightness, bulk and surface
  • Sheet size or reel width, core and grain direction
  • Binding, folding, scoring and cover construction
  • Packing, humidity protection and delivery schedule

Available Processing

  • Sheet cutting
  • Reel slitting
  • Ream packing
  • Pallet packing
  • Sample matching
