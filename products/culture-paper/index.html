High-performance surfaces for premium printing and publishing.

Culture Paper Grades

  • Woodfree Paper - Uncoated woodfree paper with high brightness, good opacity and stable runnability for books, notebooks and commercial printing.
  • Color Offset Paper - Lightweight colored offset paper for office forms, inserts, publishing, stationery and promotional printing.
  • LWC Paper (Lightweight Coated) - Lightweight coated paper for magazines, catalogs, inserts, manuals and high-volume commercial printing.
  • NCR Paper (Carbonless Paper) - Self-copying carbonless paper for forms, invoices, and receipts — no carbon sheet needed, clean and smudge-free.
  • Copy Paper - Office copy paper in multiple grammages, sheet formats and packing options for distributors, institutions and private-label buyers.
  • Newsprint - Cost-efficient, high-speed printing paper for newspapers, advertising inserts, and mass-circulation publications.
  • A4 Copy Paper - Standard A4 office copy paper — bright white, jam-free, and laser/inkjet compatible in bulk packaging for export.
  • A4 Thermal Paper - A4-sized thermal paper for portable and mobile printing — no ink, no toner, just heat. Ideal for on-the-go professionals.
  • Thermal Paper Roll - Small-format thermal paper rolls for POS terminals, receipt printers, and instant photo printers — crisp images, reliable performance.
  • Tracing Paper - Translucent tracing paper for luxury packaging, printing, drawing, invitations and decorative applications.
