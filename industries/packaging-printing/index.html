Packaging solutions

Packaging & Printing Paper Solutions

Packaging performance starts with matching the board structure to the pack format, printing method and converting line. YOUNGSUN helps box makers, printers and packaging distributors compare structural boards, printable boards and wrapping papers as one coordinated sourcing program.

Performance Priorities

Structure

Stiffness, thickness and dimensional stability for the finished pack.

Print surface

Smoothness, brightness and coating matched to the artwork and press.

Converting

Reliable creasing, die-cutting, folding, gluing and wrapping performance.

Recommended Paper Grades

  • Grey Board - FSC-certified recycled grey board for rigid boxes, bookbinding, stationery, puzzles and industrial packaging.
  • Folding Box Board - C1S ivory board with a smooth coated surface, high brightness and reliable folding performance for printed cartons.
  • Duplex Board - Coated white-top duplex board with grey back for economical folding cartons and printed packaging.
  • Kraft Paper - Natural kraft paper for sacks, bags, wrapping, industrial packaging and paper converting.
  • C2S Art Board Card - Coated-two-sides art board with bright white surface and smooth finish — for premium print and high-end card applications.
  • Black Paper - Deep-black paper and cardboard for luxury packaging, hang tags, stationery, printing and creative converting.

Buyer Checklist

  • Finished packaging format and load requirement
  • Target GSM, caliper or board thickness
  • Sheet size, grain direction or reel width
  • Printing, lamination and surface finishing method
  • Die-cutting, creasing, folding and gluing process
  • Order volume, packing method and destination port

Available Processing

  • Sheet cutting
  • Die-cutting
  • Lamination
  • Embossing
  • Export packing
